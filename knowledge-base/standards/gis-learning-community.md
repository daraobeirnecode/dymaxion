# GIS Learning and Community Resources

Trusted long-running blogs: Paul Ramsey (blog.cleverelephant.ca — PostGIS internals and spatial SQL judgment), Bill Dollins (blog.geomusings.com — pragmatic geo-architecture), the Crunchy Data / Crunchy Bridge spatial posts (much of the best modern PostGIS writing), Anita Graser (anitagraser.com — QGIS, movement data, Trajectools), and the opengeos ecosystem around Qiusheng Wu (leafmap, geemap, open-source geospatial Python). Conferences that matter: FOSS4G (global OSGeo conference, plus regionals FOSS4G-NA and FOSS4G Europe — talk archives on the FOSS4G YouTube channels are a free education), Esri User Conference (San Diego, July — the ArcGIS roadmap event, with the Developer Summit in Palm Springs for API depth), State of the Map (OpenStreetMap Foundation), and Cloud-Native Geospatial conference/forum events for COG/STAC/GeoParquet developments. Community chat: OSGeo Slack (osgeo-slack invite via osgeo.org) with active #postgis and #gdal channels, the MapLibre Slack for the vector-rendering stack, The Spatial Community Slack (large general geo community), deck.gl's Slack for large-scale WebGL viz, and GIS StackExchange (gis.stackexchange.com) as the durable Q&A archive. Newsletters worth the inbox: WeeklyOSM (OpenStreetMap ecosystem digest), James Killick's Map Happenings, Spatial Awareness (industry commentary), and the Cloud-Native Geospatial newsletter for format/spec movement. Foundation and doc anchors: OSGeo.org project pages, postgis.net/docs, gdal.org, docs.qgis.org, developers.arcgis.com, and OGC's ogc.org/standards for primary specs. Podcasts/video: MapScaping podcast (excellent practitioner interviews across ESRI and OSS topics) and Esri's ArcGIS blogs for release-by-release changes. Practical guidance for staying current: skim WeeklyOSM + Spatial Awareness weekly, watch FOSS4G talk uploads quarterly, and read release notes for PostGIS/GDAL/QGIS/ArcGIS Pro on each major release rather than relying on secondhand summaries.
